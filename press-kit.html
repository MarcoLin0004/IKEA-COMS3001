About the Campaign

A Cross-Media Lifestyle Collaboration

Home of Happiness is a cross-media collaboration campaign between IKEA and Sanrio designed to connect with younger audiences through emotional branding, immersive room aesthetics, and social media culture.

The campaign introduces themed IKEA room displays inspired by popular Sanrio characters including Kuromi, Cinnamoroll, and Pompompurin.

By combining IKEA's functional home solutions with Sanrio's emotionally engaging character culture, the collaboration transforms living spaces into expressive and personalised environments.

Campaign Objectives

Strategic Communication Goals

Reach new Gen Z audiences

Designed for Gen Z Living

Audience Profile

The campaign primarily targets Gen Z consumers aged 18-27 who value aesthetics, identity expression, and digital culture. These audiences are highly active on TikTok and Instagram, and are influenced by visually engaging, emotionally relatable content.

Character-Based Aesthetic Segments

Three Emotional Room Directions

Cinnamoroll

Soft and calming aesthetics for gentle, bright, comforting spaces.

Pompompurin

Cozy and comforting aesthetics built around warmth, softness, and everyday joy.

Kuromi

Playful, expressive styling for bolder collector rooms with attitude and contrast.
