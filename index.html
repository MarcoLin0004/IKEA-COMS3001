Collector's Room Drop

Turn your small space into your personal collection world.

Your room is more than a place to live. It is where your interests, collections, and identity come together. Collector's Room Drop is an IKEA x IP collaboration designed to help young renters and collectors create affordable, flexible, and display-ready living spaces.
