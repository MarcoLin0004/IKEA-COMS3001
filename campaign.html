Campaign Story

A Room That Shows Who You Are

Collector's Room Drop reimagines small-space living as a form of self-expression, display, and digital identity.

Audience Insight

Why This Campaign?

For many young people, a room is no longer just a functional living space. It is a personal stage, a display zone, and a digital identity space. From collectible figures and blind boxes to desk setups and shelf styling, small rooms have become places where lifestyle, fandom, and self-expression meet.

Concept

The Campaign Concept

Collector's Room Drop responds to this shift by combining IKEA's modular home solutions with the emotional appeal of IP culture. Instead of treating furniture only as practical objects, the campaign presents IKEA products as tools for personal curation, display, and creative living.

Small space. Big personality. Designed by IKEA. Styled by you.

Strategic Pillars

Key Ideas

Small-Space Living

Young renters often need flexible, affordable, and compact room solutions.

Collection Culture

Collectibles are not only products; they are emotional objects and identity markers.

Digital Identity

A well-styled room can become part of how young audiences present themselves online.

IKEA Relevance

IKEA can move beyond functional furniture and become part of youth lifestyle culture.

Journey

Campaign Journey

  1. Discover

    Audiences first encounter the campaign through TikTok and Instagram teasers.

  2. Explore

    They visit the website to understand the collaboration and browse room ideas.

  3. Participate

    They join the #MyCollectorRoom Challenge by sharing their own styled space.

  4. Connect

    Selected posts are featured, creating a sense of community and campaign continuity.
